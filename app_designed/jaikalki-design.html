PHOTO HALO · GOLD STATS · ROLE BADGE
Theme · Light Mode
Warm by default
Surfaces use #FAFAF7 warm white instead of pure white, and #FDF8EE cream for cards. This subtle warmth ties everything back to the logo's parchment-like background — never cold or sterile.
Theme · Dark Mode
Brown, not black
Dark mode uses #1A0F08 as the deepest surface (very dark brown) instead of pure black. Surface elevation steps up through #2D1810. Gold becomes brighter (#FFD66B) for visibility on dark.
Hierarchy · Buttons
Gold = action
Primary CTAs always use the gold gradient with brown text (high contrast, ~5.2:1). Secondary buttons are brown outline with brown text. Text buttons use gold-dark for inline links. Never use gold for destructive actions — that's crimson territory.
Hierarchy · Cards
Gold left border
Stat cards and important content use a 4px gold left border on a warm-white background. This subtle accent feels premium without being gaudy — exactly like a touch of gold-leaf on traditional ledger paper.
Roles · Badges
Subtle differentiation
Role colors appear only in the avatar badge and the role chip — never as full-screen tints. This keeps the brand brown-and-gold dominant while still letting an admin's red avatar stand out from a manager's blue.
Typography · Pairing
Playfair × Poppins
Playfair Display for headlines mirrors the elegant serif of the Jaikalki wordmark. Poppins for body keeps modern HRMS data clean and scannable. Italic Playfair on accent words (like welcome) creates the brand's signature warmth.
